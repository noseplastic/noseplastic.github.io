Privacy policy

This Google OAuth client is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • It accesses only files the client itself creates in the owner's Google Drive (drive.file scope).
  • It collects no data from anyone else, and it does not sell or share Google user data.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computers and environments.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
